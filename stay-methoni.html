where we stayed in Methoni

PETRA APARTMENTS, METHONI

A beautifully designed, stylish two-storey apartment with a mezzanine bedroom, luxurious interiors and a gorgeous private garden. Thoughtful touches, excellent facilities and a warm welcome make this a particularly special place to stay.

★★★★★4-star stay

ABOUT THE PROPERTY

Location

Ideally situated in Methoni, approximately a 5-minute walk from the local cafés and tavernas and around 10 minutes from the beach. A great base for exploring the south-west Peloponnese, including Pylos, Koroni, Finikounda and the beautiful Voidokilia Beach.

Parking

Conveniently located for exploring the area by car.

The accommodation

Petra is a standalone, two-storey property with a clever mezzanine bedroom overlooking the living area. The interiors are beautifully styled, with elegant neutral colours, natural textures, bronze fittings and gorgeous statement lighting.

The kitchen is exceptionally well equipped, the lounge is comfortable and the bathroom features a rather impressive carved stone basin.

Outside, there's a lovely private garden with hanging egg chairs, dining furniture, lush planting and an outdoor shower tucked between the greenery.

Our host had also thoughtfully provided fresh eggs, butter, jam, tea, coffee, fruit and even a bottle of wine. A lovely welcome after a long journey.

one thing to know

The mezzanine bedroom has a rather low ceiling above the bed. Not a problem if you're vertically challenged, but if you're over 6ft, you'll need to remember to duck!

How long to stay

Perfect for a few nights or a longer stay. The excellent facilities, comfortable living space and central location make it an ideal base for exploring this corner of the Peloponnese.

would we stay again?

Absolutely! Petra Apartments was one of those rare finds that exceeded expectations. Beautifully designed, wonderfully equipped and with a gorgeous outdoor space, it really does feel like a little slice of luxury.

We only dropped a star because of the low headroom in the mezzanine bedroom, which might prove awkward for taller guests. Otherwise, we'd happily return. Just remember one thing if you're tall – duck!
