PRIVACY POLICY

Including our cookie policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

This policy explains what information Just Stay Away (juststayaway.com) collects when you visit our website, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Just Stay Away is run by Max and Jacquie, trading as Just Stay Away, based in the United Kingdom. We are responsible for your personal data under UK data protection law (UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018).

1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

Information you give us. If you email us, use our contact form or sign up to our newsletter, we collect your name, your email address and anything you choose to tell us.

Information collected automatically. With your consent, we use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site. This includes the pages you visit, how long you stay, the type of device and browser you use, your approximate location (country or city) and how you found us. Google Analytics does not tell us who you are. We also use Vercel Web Analytics, which counts visits without using cookies or identifying you.

Affiliate link tracking. If you click a link to a booking site, the partner may record that you came from our site so we can be credited if you book. See section 5.

2. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION, AND OUR LEGAL BASIS

  • To reply to your messages (legitimate interests: answering people who contact us).
  • To send our newsletter, only if you have signed up (consent). Every email has an unsubscribe link.
  • To understand and improve the website with analytics (consent, given through our cookie banner).
  • To earn commission through affiliate links (consent, given through our cookie banner).
  • To comply with the law where we have to (legal obligation).

3. WHO WE SHARE IT WITH

We do not sell, trade or rent your personal information. We use a small number of trusted services to run the site, and they process data on our behalf:

  • Vercel – hosts the website and provides cookie-free visitor counts.
  • Google Analytics (Google) – website statistics.
  • CookieYes – records your cookie choices.
  • Web3Forms – handles messages sent through our contact form.
  • Kit – stores newsletter sign-ups and sends our emails (only if you sign up).
  • YouTube (Google) – plays the videos embedded on our pages.
  • Affiliate partners – see section 5.

Some of these companies are based outside the UK, for example in the USA. Where your data is transferred abroad, they use safeguards approved under UK law, such as the UK–US Data Bridge or standard contractual clauses. We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the rights or safety of our website, our visitors or others.

4. HOW LONG WE KEEP IT

  • Emails and contact-form messages: up to 2 years, then deleted.
  • Newsletter sign-ups: until you unsubscribe.
  • Google Analytics data: 14 months.

5. AFFILIATE LINKS

Just Stay Away is free to read. To help cover our costs, some links to accommodation, car hire, ferries and tours are affiliate links. If you book through one, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. This never changes what we recommend: we only include places we have stayed in or used ourselves.

We currently work with Stay22 (for Booking.com, Expedia and other booking sites) and Travelpayouts. When you click one of these links, the partner (or the booking site) may set a cookie to record the referral. Their own privacy policies explain how they use your data.

6. COOKIES AND SIMILAR TECHNOLOGIES

Cookies are small text files stored by your browser. When you first visit, our cookie banner lets you accept or reject each type of cookie except the necessary ones:

  • Necessary – needed for the site to work and to remember your cookie choices. Always on.
  • Functional – features such as embedded YouTube videos and social sharing.
  • Analytics – Google Analytics statistics (see section 1).
  • Performance – help us understand how well the site is working.
  • Advertisement – affiliate link tracking (see section 5).

Analytics, functional, performance and advertisement cookies are only used if you allow them. You can change your mind at any time using the cookie settings button in the bottom corner of every page. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

Trip planner ticks and countdown: on our trip planner page, the boxes you tick and the flight date you enter for your countdown are saved in your own browser (using local storage) so they're still there when you come back. This stays on your device, is never sent to us or anyone else, and is used only to remember your ticks and your date. You can remove it at any time with the "Clear my ticks" and "Clear my date" buttons on that page or by clearing your browser data. The calendar reminders file is made in your browser too: nothing is sent to us.

7. LINKS TO OTHER WEBSITES

Our site links to other websites we do not own or control, including booking sites. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices, so please read their privacy policies.

8. CHILDREN

Our website is not aimed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you think a child has given us their information, please contact us and we will delete it.

9. YOUR RIGHTS

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct or delete it;
  • object to, or ask us to restrict, how we use it;
  • ask for your data in a portable format;
  • withdraw your consent at any time (for example, by unsubscribing or changing your cookie settings).

To use any of these rights, email us at the address below. We will reply within one month. If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

10. KEEPING YOUR INFORMATION SAFE

We take sensible technical and organisational steps to protect your information from loss, misuse and unauthorised access.

11. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may update this policy from time to time, for example when we join a new affiliate programme. The date at the top shows when it last changed.

12. CONTACT US

If you have any questions about this policy or your information, email us at juststayawaytravel@gmail.com or use our contact form.
