where we stayed in Agios Stefanos

DAMIANOS APARTMENTS, AGIOS STEFANOS

Spacious, self-catering apartments with a lovely pool, peaceful setting and easy access to the waterfront. An excellent base for exploring the north-east coast of Corfu.

★★★★★5-star stay

ABOUT THE PROPERTY

Location

Situated in Agios Stefanos, on the east coast of Corfu, approximately 5km from Kassiopi (around 5 minutes by car). A lovely alternative to staying in Kassiopi itself, with a quieter village atmosphere and plenty of waterfront restaurants and cafés.

Parking

Ample parking within the gated property, making it particularly convenient if you're hiring a car.

The accommodation

Spacious, self-contained apartments with fully equipped cooking facilities, making them ideal for anyone wanting to prepare their own meals and enjoy a longer stay. There's also a lovely swimming pool and outdoor space for relaxing.

How long to stay

Ideal for a week or longer. The self-catering facilities, spacious accommodation and pool make it a comfortable base rather than simply somewhere to lay your head.

would we stay again?

Absolutely! It makes a great base for exploring the north-east coast, while giving you the benefit of staying in the lovely village of Agios Stefanos. With its upmarket café bars, traditional waterfront tavernas and relaxed harbour atmosphere, there's plenty to enjoy without having to venture far in the evening.
