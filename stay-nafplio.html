where we stayed in Nafplio

FILDISI LUXURY SUITES – NAFPLIO

A lovely luxury stopover, around a 20-minute walk from Nafplio town centre, with plenty of off-street parking. Ideal for a night or two while exploring the Peloponnese, with spacious suites, beautiful bathrooms, private balconies and a generous breakfast included.

★★★★★Our rating: 5 / 5

ABOUT THE ACCOMMODATION

Fildisi Luxury Suites offers stylish, modern accommodation with a touch of luxury, beautifully designed for a relaxing stay. Spacious suites feature large comfortable beds, elegant bathrooms and private balconies with lovely views across Nafplio, the sea and Palamidi Fortress. A delicious breakfast is included, making this a lovely place to start the day.

Modern, beautifully presented suites with a large king-size bed, additional mezzanine sleeping area, luxurious bathroom and complimentary toiletries. Little extras included tea, coffee and biscuits.

The view & little touches

Private balconies with outdoor seating and lovely views of the sea, Nafplio and Palamidi Fortress. Complimentary refreshments and a wonderfully welcoming breakfast experience add to the luxury.

Breakfast

Breakfast deserves a special mention! Rather than just a standard buffet, you could order freshly prepared dishes including omelettes, Greek salad, cheese, fruit, yoghurt and preserves, alongside coffee, juices and water. All served by two delightful ladies who made the experience even more enjoyable. A real gastronomic delight!

Why we chose it

We chose Fildisi Luxury Suites as a convenient stopover on our journey to explore other parts of the Peloponnese. It was a little more luxurious than our usual accommodation and looked perfect for a one- or two-night stay.

would we stay again?

Absolutely! A lovely touch of luxury, spacious rooms, wonderful breakfast and a welcoming atmosphere. Perfect for a short stay when visiting Nafplio.
