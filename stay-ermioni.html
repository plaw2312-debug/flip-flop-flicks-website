where we stayed in Ermioni

DIMARAKIS COLLECTION – ROCK N SUN

A lovely-looking frontage, newly refurbished accommodation and plenty of private parking. A great find for our stay in Ermioni.

★★★★★Our rating: 5 / 5

ABOUT THE PLACE

We stayed at Dimarakis Collection – Rock N Sun, a modern, newly refurbished apartment in Ermioni, offering plenty of space and everything we needed for a comfortable stay. The decor was contemporary, the rooms were spacious and the apartment was exceptionally well equipped. It's a 5-minute drive down to the harbour, or a 10-minute walk.

There was a lovely outdoor terrace with shaded seating, perfect for breakfast or a relaxing drink, overlooking the surrounding pine trees. Inside, we had a fully equipped kitchen, comfortable bedroom, spacious living area, powerful shower, washing machine and air conditioning. Little extras from our host, Konstantinos, including honey, jam, crackers and a fridge stocked with 24 bottles of water, were a thoughtful welcome.

would we stay again?

Absolutely! A great location for exploring the area, with a modern, clean and comfortable apartment that has everything you need, whether you're staying for a few nights or a longer break.
