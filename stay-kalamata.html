where we stayed in Kalamata

SATORI STUDIO, KALAMATA

A newly built, modern and well-equipped studio apartment with a spacious balcony, comfortable living space and everything needed for a short stay. Convenient for the marina and city centre.

★★★★★4-star stay

ABOUT THE PROPERTY

Location

Situated close to Kalamata's marina and railway park, approximately a 15-minute walk from the seafront and main shopping area. Cafés, bakeries, supermarkets and local amenities are nearby. Kalamata Airport is around 7km away.

Parking

Free parking is available on the surrounding streets.

The accommodation

Built in 2023, the apartment is on the first floor of a modern building, with lift access. It's open-plan, modern and well presented, with a comfortable queen-size bed, plenty of wardrobe storage, a sofa, desk and Smart TV.

The kitchen is fully equipped with everything needed for preparing breakfast and light meals, while the bathroom includes a washing machine. The real bonus is the large balcony, complete with outdoor furniture and a sofa, making it a lovely spot to sit and relax.

How long to stay

Ideal for a short stay, particularly if you're exploring Kalamata or using the city as a starting point for a Peloponnese road trip. It has everything you need for a comfortable few nights.

would we stay again?

Possibly, although we'd probably look for somewhere with a little more holiday atmosphere next time. The apartment itself was excellent – modern, spacious, comfortable and very well equipped.

We dropped a star purely because of its location. It's tucked away within a large residential area and doesn't quite have the holiday feel we look for when travelling.

That said, if you're happy to be based in a residential neighbourhood, it's a very good apartment and offers comfortable accommodation close to Kalamata's marina.
