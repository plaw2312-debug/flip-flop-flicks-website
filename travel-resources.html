INSURANCE

Insurance is the dull bit, but it's what saves a holiday when something goes wrong. Buy travel insurance as soon as you've booked, and think about car hire excess cover before you reach the rental desk. Always read the policy wording: we can tell you what we use, but we're not insurance advisers.

Cover4Rentals

Car hire excess insurance for UK residents, as single-trip or annual policies.

why we use it: We buy an annual excess policy before we travel. For us it costs about the same as one week's extra cover at the hire desk.

QUESTIONS WE GET ASKED

Do you get paid if I book through these links?

Sometimes. Some of the links on this page are affiliate links, so if you book through them we may earn a small commission. It costs you nothing extra, and it helps keep Just Stay Away and our YouTube channel going. We only list sites we use ourselves or would happily use.

Do I need a car in Greece?

Not for the islands' main towns or Athens, but on the mainland, and especially in the Peloponnese, a car makes all the difference. Many of our favourite beaches and villages have little or no public transport.

Should I buy car hire excess insurance before I travel?

That's what we do. We buy an annual car hire excess policy before we go, which for us costs about the same as one week's extra cover at the hire desk. Excess insurance usually pays you back after a claim rather than removing the excess at the desk, so you'll still need enough credit for the deposit. Check the policy wording before you buy.

What's the easiest way to book ferries in Greece?

We use Ferryhopper to compare routes and times across the ferry companies and book the tickets in one place. Book early for July and August, and plan your ferries before your hotels.
